Project direction

Omni is Kobe's name for the overall application and digital virtual assistant, starting with editing and distribution needs. Ollama is its planned local model-serving component, described in Kobe's own metaphor as Omni's "composure system." Claude Code is the tool for building the application.

Build a workflow that accepts video files, studies reference videos, creates a brand sheet and presents a storyboard for review before editing. After Kobe approves the finished edit, connect it to publishing across his chosen platforms.

The priority is genuinely free tools, not free trials. Kobe is open to building the publishing connection rather than paying for a cross-posting service. Instagram is a known destination; the other destinations have not been settled.
