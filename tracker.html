Project direction

The five-year vision comes first

The first three years are for defining who I am and focusing on myself: "I'm selfishly focused on myself." The next two are for creating space for other people through my art.

  • Years 1 to 3: build the car and establish my footprint, starting in Atlanta. Develop and share my paintings and music, and build the systems that support being fully seen.
  • Years 4 to 5: turn the established work and platform toward philanthropic art and cultural diplomacy, showcasing people's humanity across borders and connecting divided communities and countries.
  • The car: Blue Boy and my paintings are the body, my music is the sound system, automated editing is the engine, Permission Slips are the suspension, energy and rest are the fuel, exhibitions, publishing and rollout are the wheels, and I am the driver.
  • Where Omni fits: Omni's editing pipeline is the engine, built to cut editing time and overthinking so more energy goes to creating.

The full arc, with the 2027 destinations, is in Vision.

Omni

Omni is Kobe's name for the overall application and digital virtual assistant, starting with editing and distribution needs. Ollama is its planned local model-serving component, described in Kobe's own metaphor as Omni's "composure system." Claude Code is the tool for building the application.

Build a workflow that accepts video files, studies reference videos, creates a brand sheet and presents a storyboard for review before editing. After Kobe approves the finished edit, connect it to publishing across his chosen platforms.

The priority is genuinely free tools, not free trials. Kobe is open to building the publishing connection rather than paying for a cross-posting service. Instagram is a known destination; the other destinations have not been settled.

Vision

My five-year creative journey. The first three years are for defining who I am and focusing on myself. The next two are for creating space for other people through my art.

The declaration

"I'm selfishly focused on myself."

This declaration sits at the top of the priorities for the first three years. The focus is building my identity, my work, and the systems that support being fully seen.

Building the car

  • Body: Blue Boy and my paintings. The visible form and identity of the car, carrying the work's tenderness and expression.
  • Sound system: my music. The voice of the ride.
  • Engine: automated editing. A process that reduces editing time and overthinking, leaving more time and energy for creating.
  • Suspension: Permission Slips. Support for the transition into being fully seen, including permission to allow my body to catch up to a realization.
  • Fuel: energy and rest. What sustains the creative process without running me empty.
  • Wheels: exhibitions, publishing, and rollout. How the work moves out into the world.
  • Driver: me. I choose the direction.

The Permission Slips and automated editing are the two supports I am building now. The aim is to enjoy the transition, not overthink every part of it.

Years 1 to 3: define myself and establish my footprint

Build the machine while developing and sharing my paintings and music. Establish my presence, with Atlanta as the current focus.

Destinations named for 2027:

  • Buckhead: "Check."
  • Midtown: pursue SCAD.
  • Uptown: pursue MOCA.
  • The mayor's office: connect and develop projects together.
  • Expansion: keep exploring, then branch out regionally, nationally, and internationally.
  • Europe: the international destination I named.

These are my intended destinations, not a list of confirmed partnerships or bookings. International expansion does not have to wait for every regional or national step. After the Blue Boy series is done, I want to launch it internationally.

The conversation the work carries: Black men's tenderness can exist without explanation, performance, or defense. The paintings make room for that humanity to be encountered.

Years 4 to 5: create space for others

Turn the established artwork and platform toward philanthropic work and cultural diplomacy through art.

The vision is to connect people across borders, showcase their humanity, and contribute to connection between countries and communities that have experienced division. Art becomes a way to make room for others and help people encounter one another as human beings.

This is the aspiration for the work, not a promise that art alone can repair those divisions.

The destination

A self-directed creative life in which my paintings and music support me without consuming all my energy, followed by using that platform to give back and create space for other people.

The arc stays in relative years. I named 2027 for the destinations, without assigning exact calendar dates to every stage of the five-year plan.
